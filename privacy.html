Gib & Goob Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Gib & Goob Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Gib & Goob channels to upload the channels' own videos and to read the channels' own statistics on YouTube and TikTok.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload access: to publish our own videos to our own channel.
  • YouTube read-only and YouTube Analytics read-only access: to read our own channel's video list and performance statistics.
  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): our own TikTok account's basic profile, and permission to upload and publish our own videos to that account.

What we do with the data

  • The data is used only to publish our videos and to understand how our own channels perform.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising.
  • We do not access any other person's account or data.

Storage and security

  • Sign-in credentials are stored in Windows Credential Manager on the owner's own computer and are never published.
  • Analytics reports are kept privately on that same computer.

Removing access

  • Google: revoke this tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • TikTok: remove the app at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services permissions.
